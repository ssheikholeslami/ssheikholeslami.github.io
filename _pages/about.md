Hi there! I’m Sina Sheikholeslami, and I do research in machine learning systems and applications of machine learning and deep learning in various domains. I'm a Postdoc at [KTH Climate Action Centre](https://www.kth.se/climateactioncentre/start-kth-climate-action-centre-1.1085675) and the [Division of Energy Systems](https://www.energy.kth.se/energy-systems/), Department of Energy Technology at KTH, working with [Francesco Fuso-Nerini](https://www.kth.se/profile/ffn) and [Ricardo Vinuesa](https://www.vinuesalab.com/principal-investigator/) on leveraging AI for Sustainable Development. I primarily work on the [Beyond 2030](https://www.digitalfutures.kth.se/project/beyond-2030-achieving-the-sdgs-within-the-planetary-boundaries-an-ai-based-approach-2/) project, and also contribute to [OnStove](https://onstove-documentation.readthedocs.io/en/latest/index.html#).

I did my [PhD studies](https://urn.kb.se/resolve?urn=urn:nbn:se:kth:diva-360720) in the Distributed Computing Group of the Division of Software and Computer Systems (SCS), EECS School of KTH, where I was advised by Vladimir Vlassov, Amir Payberah, and Jim Dowling. My main focus was at the intersection of distributed systems, machine learning/deep learning, and data-intensive computing, as well as applications of ML/DL in different domains.

I have a particular interest in finding (relatively) simple yet sound and practical solutions to challenging questions in machine learning, deep learning, and scalable computing. I also strongly believe in open science and reproducible research, and to that end all of my own work is publicly available with ready-to-execute scripts for reproducing the results.
